About Novaspark

Who We Are

Novaspark Games is an independent studio founded by a small team with a big ambition: to make games that matter. We believe in tight storytelling, satisfying mechanics, and worlds that reward curiosity.

Our Vision

Every game we make starts with a question; what would make a player feel genuinely surprised, moved, or proud? We design backwards from that feeling, building systems and stories that deliver it consistently.

Our Technology

We work across proprietary and industry-standard engines to ship polished titles on PC, console, and mobile. Accessibility and performance aren't afterthoughts — they're built into our pipeline from day one.
