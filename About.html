Our Story

Novaspark Games began as a two-person project in a shared flat, built on strong coffee and a conviction that independent studios can compete with the biggest names in the industry, on one condition; boldness.

Core Values

What Drives Us

Innovation First

We set out to build things we haven't seen before. Our internal engine gives us the freedom to prototype rapidly and ship mechanics that feel genuinely fresh, not borrowed from last year's bestseller.

Player-Centric

Our playtesting process is ruthless. If a feature doesn't make the experience better for the person holding the controller, it doesn't ship. Your time and intelligence are never taken for granted.

Artistic Integrity

From the moment you boot a Novaspark title, every visual, every note, every line of dialogue has been through multiple rounds of creative review. We don't compromise on craft.
